Research Statement

I am interested in designing reliable, scalable, and secure distributed systems. My undergraduate research experience focuses on how decentralized systems achieve consistency, efficiency, and auditability under dynamic network environments.

Through continuous research training since my first undergraduate year, I have participated in the complete research pipeline, including problem formulation, protocol design, system implementation, experimental deployment, performance evaluation, and academic writing.

Blockchain Systems Consensus mechanisms, dynamic validator membership, blockchain forensics, and auditable verification.
Network Measurement Ethereum P2P discovery, topology reconstruction, encrypted communication analysis, and large-scale measurement.
Smart Contract Security Cross-contract vulnerability analysis, dependency modeling, and automated detection methods.
Scalable Distributed Systems Layer-2 scalability, synchronization mechanisms, and incentive security in decentralized networks.

Research Advisor: Associate Professor Teng Huang, Guangzhou University.

Research Projects

My undergraduate research mainly focuses on blockchain systems and distributed network security. I have contributed to protocol design, system implementation, experimental evaluation, and academic publication.

Blockchain Consensus and Dynamic Membership

Research Member · National Key R&D Program-related Project

Studied validator admission, state synchronization, auditable certification, and Byzantine fault tolerance in blockchain systems with dynamic membership.

  • Participated in membership update, admission control, and synchronous witness mechanism design;
  • Studied traceable validator behavior and forensic evidence generation;
  • Contributed to implementation, formal analysis, and wide-area evaluation.

Ethereum P2P Measurement and Topology Reconstruction

Project Leader · Discv4 Topology Reconstruction / Core Member · Discv5 Measurement

Focused on Ethereum node discovery, encrypted session analysis, topology reconstruction, and network measurement.

  • Designed the Discv4 measurement architecture and data collection pipeline;
  • Built topology storage and analysis systems using Neo4j and Docker;
  • Analyzed Discv5 handshake procedures and ECDH-based key management.

Smart Contract Security and Vulnerability Detection

First Author Research Project

Developed vulnerability detection methods based on complex network analysis and heterogeneous graph learning for interactive smart-contract scenarios.

  • Modeled cross-contract calls and state dependencies;
  • Designed graph-based vulnerability representations;
  • Achieved 92.81%–94.87% accuracy across multiple vulnerability detection tasks.
